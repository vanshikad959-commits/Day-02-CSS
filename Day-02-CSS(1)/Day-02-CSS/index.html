About Us

Founded in 2024 in Bengaluru, NovaByte Solutions is a team of developers, designers and problem-solvers. We partner with startups and small businesses to turn ideas into reliable digital products, following modern web standards from day one.

Our Mission

To make high-quality, accessible technology affordable for every growing business. Learn more about the standards we follow at the W3C Web Accessibility Initiative.
